## Why node-zugferd?

Electronic invoicing is no longer optional. Public-sector buyers and a growing share of private ones already require ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, or EN 16931 compliant invoices, and regulation keeps pushing the rest of the market the same way. If your product issues invoices, compliance has become part of the job.

The difficulty is that the standard is not a single format. It is a family of profiles, a large XML schema, a set of business rules, and a PDF/A-3 container that has to carry the XML and metadata correctly. Most teams reach for a Java microservice built on libraries like Mustang. That means standing up, deploying, and operating another service just to produce a document, then bridging it back into a TypeScript codebase.

node-zugferd exists so you don't have to. It treats e-invoicing as one typed workflow rather than a pile of formats: a single API covers the whole document lifecycle, one data model spans the profiles, and the specification details stay out of your application code. You get compliance without needing an in-house e-invoicing specialist, and you stay free to self-host, extend, and deploy wherever your app already runs.
