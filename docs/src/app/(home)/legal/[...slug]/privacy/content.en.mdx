We operate a documentation website. We do not offer user accounts or process payment information through it.

| What may be processed | Why and legal basis |

## Cookies and browser storage

The documentation uses the `sidebar_state` cookie for seven days to remember your sidebar preference. It also stores the feedback form's state in browser local storage. We do not use these items for advertising or cross-site tracking.

StackBlitz and Sandpack are third-party services. When an interactive example loads, these providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their privacy policies. We do not control this storage. You can avoid the associated data transfer by not opening interactive examples.

## Who receives data

- **Vercel** hosts the website and may process visitor request data and service logs. Vercel acts as a processor for data it handles on our instructions where the DPA applies. See [Vercel's Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice) and [Data Processing Addendum](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa).
- **StackBlitz** provides the embedded online editor. See [StackBlitz's Privacy Policy](https://stackblitz.com/privacy-policy).
- **CodeSandbox** provides Sandpack, the interactive code editor and preview used in some examples. See [CodeSandbox's Privacy Policy](https://codesandbox.io/legal/privacy).
- **GitHub** receives feedback submitted through the documentation feedback feature and hosts it in GitHub Discussions. Discussions in the public node-zugferd repository can be visible to the public. See [GitHub's General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use feedback submitted through this site to train our own AI models.

## International data transfers

The operator is based in Germany, and the providers above may process data in the United States or other countries. Where Vercel processes personal data as our processor, its DPA sets out the applicable transfer safeguards, which may include Standard Contractual Clauses. StackBlitz, CodeSandbox, and GitHub handle data under their own terms and privacy commitments. Review their linked notices for details about their locations and transfer mechanisms.

## How long we keep data

- Sidebar preference: up to seven days in the cookie. Browser local storage remains until cleared or removed by the site.
- Feedback: retained in GitHub Discussions until the discussion or comment is removed. You can ask us to remove feedback by emailing [service@jsolano.de](mailto:service@jsolano.de). Public copies, quotes, or cached versions may remain outside our control.
- Hosting and security logs: retention is controlled by Vercel's service and configuration. We do not have a separately verified retention period for these logs, so see [Vercel's Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice) for its practices.
- Data held by StackBlitz, CodeSandbox, or GitHub: governed by each provider's own retention practices.

## Your rights

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal data, request its correction or deletion, and request restrictions on its processing. You may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to receive certain data in a portable format.

To exercise your rights, email [service@jsolano.de](mailto:service@jsolano.de). We may ask for information needed to verify your request. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, work, or believe a violation took place. Your rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

For information processed directly by StackBlitz, CodeSandbox, GitHub, or Vercel for their own purposes, you may need to contact that provider directly.
